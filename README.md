A [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry for **Make the AI Judge Laugh** — one joke about office life, written to make an AI judge laugh.

## The joke

> I asked for a standing desk. They gave me a meeting where I stand and present everyone else's work.

Why it lands: it's true to office life (the equipment request, the meeting that eats your time, presenting work that isn't yours), the turn on the word "standing" is surprising, and it's one tight line that reads aloud in a single breath.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. The joke is 99 characters — inside the 200-character limit.

Run it locally:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
